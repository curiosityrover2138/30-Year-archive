Bezout's theorem-> If a and b are any non zero integers, then gcd(a,b) exists and there exists 
integers x0 and y0 such that gcd(a,b)= ax0 + by0 

Fermat's little theorem-> If p is a prime then a^p = a (mod) p
This also means that a^p-1 = 1 mod p 


Fermat's numbers-> Integers of the form (2)^(2^n) + 1 

Primes among the fermat numbers i.e. numbers which are both fermat numbers and primes are called fermat primes 

Theorem-> Given any integers a and b with a=/=0 there exist unique integers q and r with b = qa+r and 
0<=r<|a|. If a !| b then 0<r<|a|
