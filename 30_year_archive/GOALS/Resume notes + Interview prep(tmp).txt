Starrocks vs Clickhouse

Starrocks-> 


Features more cost based optimization as compared to Clickhouse. So we chose this based 
on cost optimization as well as flexibility in comparision.

Healthbase frequently runs high cardinality aggregations during joins. 
Starrocks supports both Shared nothing, and shared data architecture. But in starrocks 
for healthbase, we use shared nothing architecture as it distributes data across nodes without issues.

In healthbase, we do joins based on icd, cpt or hcpc codes. And there are around 14k cpt codes and 10k icd codes 
and the combinations among them make the queries even more complicated. So we wanted an architecture that supports 
high cardinality joins and aggregations without issues.

In healthbase, aggregations often involve distinct counts, and sums of those counts for distinct patient id, claims id 
or provider id.

Tablets are stored in buckets.
Each bucket is physically stored as one or more tablets.

If replication number is 3, the tablet is replicated 3 times across nodes. 
This makes starrocks very feasible for our workload, because we can just add or remove nodes based on data increase. And this 
also saves us cost in different environments by using different replication factors.
